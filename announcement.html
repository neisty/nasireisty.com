Open Positions

I am actively looking for motivated students to join my research group at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. If you are interested in conducting research in any of the following areas, please feel free to reach out.

  • Software Engineering — empirical methods, developer behavior, code review
  • Software Quality Assurance — testing, reliability, reproducibility
  • Software Security — vulnerability detection, secure coding practices
  • Machine Learning for SE — AI-assisted testing, bug detection, automation

The lab also warmly welcomes Fulbright scholars, visiting researchers, postdoctoral fellows, and international collaborators worldwide.

Send a brief email introducing yourself, your background, and your research interests to neisty@utk.edu. I am happy to chat.
